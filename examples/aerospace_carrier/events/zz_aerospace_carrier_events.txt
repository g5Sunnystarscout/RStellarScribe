#   containerWindowTypes declared in interface/zz_aerospace_carrier_gui.gui, exactly as
#   vanilla's enclave windows do (events/nomads_events_1.txt:2291). The three action buttons
#   inside that window are effectbuttonTypes pointing at common/button_effects/, where `this`
#   is the selected object - i.e. the carrier the player had selected.
# ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
